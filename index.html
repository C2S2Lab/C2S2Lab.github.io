About

The Critical Computational Social Science (C2S2) Lab studies political communication, polarization, and misinformation using computational and mixed methods, guided by critical and social science theory. The lab examines how political information and AI-generated imagery circulate on social media and shape public understanding, and develops open computational tools — including topic modeling and visual analysis packages — to support this kind of research more broadly.

C2S2 is directed by Ayse D. Lokmanoglu, Assistant Professor of Emerging Media Studies at Boston University's College of Communication.

Research

  • Political Communication — polarization, misinformation, conspiracy theories, and agenda-setting in the U.S. and beyond
  • Computational Methods — topic modeling, visual analysis, and large language models for studying text and image data at scale
  • Visual & Multimodal Communication — how images, AI-generated content, and data visualizations shape political meaning-making
  • Crisis & Global Communication — public diplomacy, extremism, and health communication under conditions of conflict and distrust
